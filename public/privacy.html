Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 14, 2026

1. Data We Access

When you connect your Google Health / Fitbit account, Healthspan requests read-only access to your activity, sleep, heart rate, VO₂ max, and profile age data strictly to calculate your Biological Age and Pace of Aging dashboard.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

Healthspan is stateless and does not store your health metrics or OAuth tokens in any server-side database. Your OAuth session token is stored only in an encrypted, authenticated HttpOnly browser cookie, and your computed dashboard summary is cached in this tab’s sessionStorage until you disconnect or close the tab.

3. Data Sharing & Deletion

We never sell, rent, or share your Google Health data with third parties. Click Disconnect to clear the app session and this tab’s cached summary. Revoke the Google authorization grant separately via your Google Account Permissions.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

Healthspan's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
