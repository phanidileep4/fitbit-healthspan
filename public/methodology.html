Reference guidance checked November 14, 2026. These are general adult comparisons, not personal diagnoses. Your age, symptoms, medicines, training, and measurement method can change what is appropriate.

How the experimental model works

Model: wellness-heuristic-v3. Each pillar maps a baseline measurement to an app score and a hypothetical year adjustment using hand-selected piecewise-linear control points. The model sums year adjustments with chronological age, with a minimum output age of 18. A separate set of recent-measurement coefficients produces an index clamped to 0.45–1.65. These coefficients are not derived from a validated biological clock, mortality model, or WHOOP algorithm.

Source-backed comparison guidance does not validate the model coefficients. The app score averages available pillar scores, so different available pillars can produce misleading comparisons. Goals and reference ranges do not change the model. In particular, its RHR, body composition, and VO₂ coefficients are not demographic clinical reference ranges.

The baseline uses 180 completed local days and the recent window uses 30. Daily pillars need 14 distinct recorded days; VO₂ requires one. Overall age/index outputs need six available pillars. These are display rules, not clinical validation criteria. No score should replace interpretation of the actual measurements.

Coverage, history, and privacy

Green calendar cells mean a dated observation was recorded, including an explicit zero. Gray cells mean no observation; they do not mean zero activity. Exercise calendars show logged exercise days, not proof of complete training history. A manual lean-mass fallback has no date, so it does not fill calendar cells.

History is opt-in and stored only in this browser, scoped to your Google Health account when the API supplies a canonical profile identifier. Otherwise it is scoped to the current OAuth session and may not carry across reconnections. A snapshot records a successful live sync, its model version, coverage, and profile settings. It cannot backfill earlier ages.

Comparisons require different snapshot days, the same model, profile settings, measurement sources, and available pillars, with similar recorded-day counts. Matching counts do not eliminate all sampling bias. Old and new coefficients must never be silently mixed.

At most 180 snapshots are retained. Disabling saving stops new saves; Clear saved history or Disconnect deletes saved snapshots for the current account. Saved goals remain until browser site data is cleared. Browser history is not encrypted at rest. Exports contain measurements without OAuth credentials; downloaded copies must be deleted separately.
